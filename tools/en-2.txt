300|Arqan tartys
301|Two teams on phones. Each correct answer pulls the rope, a fast one pulls harder. The force is divided by team size, so unequal teams compete fairly. You win when the ribbon reaches the line or on points.
302|Teams in turn
303|Odd questions go to the first team, even ones to the second. A team’s points = the average of its players’ points, so team size does not matter.
304|Everyone for themselves
305|The whole class answers every question. Points: up to 500 for correctness and up to 500 for speed, with a streak bonus. You can grade open answers yourself: ✓, ½ or ✗.
306|No phones. Two students at the board, each with their own questions and a digital keypad. A mistake means a 1.2-second pause.
